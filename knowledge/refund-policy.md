A customer may return an item within 30 days of its purchase and receive a
refund within 3 working days of the return arriving. Faulty goods are exempt
from the 30-day window.[^owner-interview-2026-09-05]

## Return window

The return window is 30 days, counted from the purchase date. A returned item
must come back in its original condition.

## Refund

The refund lands within 3 working days of the return being received.

## Faulty goods

Faulty goods are exempt from the return window: they may be returned at any
time, and no separate limit is stated.
